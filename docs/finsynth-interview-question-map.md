This is the working bank behind the case study and “Ask me.” It is deliberately a map, not a script: **Ready** means the case already supports a truthful answer; **Need Ritika** means it needs your lived detail before it belongs on the site. Do not turn the latter into polished claims yet.

## How to use this map

1. Answer every **Need Ritika** question in your own words—rough notes are enough.
2. I will turn only supported answers into “Ask me” entries and link them to the relevant case-study section.
3. Keep pilot discoveries as discoveries. A thoughtful unanswered question is better than a manufactured result.
